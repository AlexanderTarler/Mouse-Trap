ROUND 8 PLAN — gameplay and UX (agreed 2026-10-16)
==================================================
In this order (cheap and self-contained first; the microphone last).

1  Headphones line.
   The opening's first card: "Best played with headphones." (small, under
   the title, fades with it). Directional hearing is a core skill here.

2  The mimicry has a tell.
   EntityAudio.mimic(): what it copies comes back slightly off, as Nest
   Gamma's do: pitched down a little (0.86-0.93, never the player's own 1.0)
   and, for footsteps, the rhythm wrong (four steps, one late). Attentive
   players can learn to doubt a familiar sound.

3  The low-fuel warning.
   Below 15% the generator coughs now and then (its loop dips in pitch and
   volume for a moment) and the cabin's lit rooms brown out briefly
   (CabinLight.presence_flicker), more often as it nears empty. Then the
   blackout is no surprise, and there's time to refuel.

4  A clock the player can read.
   - A wristwatch: hold [T] to raise the wrist (a small analogue watch face
     drawn in the corner, its hands at the game's time). The game's clock
     as a believable one: dawn 06:00 (progress 0.0), dusk 20:00 (0.5),
     dawn again 06:00 (1.0); so a night runs 20:00-06:00.
   - The hall clock ticks (Clock.wav, imported), louder at night.
   - Taught once (TutorialManager hint, the first night).

5  The notebook's "Entity" chapter.
   A chapter after the notes, written by the player character, empty until
   the first entry. Each lesson writes (or updates) an entry the moment it
   happens, in his words, e.g.:
     the beam        "It backed off when I held the light on it." (+ "longer
                     each time" after the second drive-off)
     lit rooms       "It won't stay in a lit room."
     sound           "It hears me. Running, doors, things I drop."
     ambush          "It waits in the dark. A breath first, then it comes."
     mimicry         "It copies sounds. Mine. They come back slightly
                     wrong."
     windows         "It tries the windows. Planks hold it for a while."
     upstairs        "It climbs. The upstairs windows aren't safe."
     the peeker      "Something at the window. Watching me."
   The first entry: a thought ("I should write this down."). Kept by the
   checkpoint like the other pages; the index lists the chapter.

6  The cabin turns on the player, night by night.
   A HouseUnease node: settling creaks, a cabinet shutting, a door handle
   tried, the old house groaning; in a room away from the player, never
   near the Entity's real position (it shouldn't lie about where it is).
   Night 1 almost nothing, Night 2 now and then (every 60-120 s), Night 3
   often (every 30-60 s). Off by day and during cutscenes. Uses
   "Old House_creeky metal and wood_ambiance_7", "Cabinet_shut",
   "Door_handle_jiggle", "Door_squeeky_2" (imported).

7  Captions (optional, off by default: Options > Captions).
   One line, bottom centre, for the sounds the game depends on: the
   ambush breath, a copied sound, knocking, glass breaking, planks giving,
   the crows going up, footsteps nearby unseen, the car alarm, the
   generator dying. With a rough direction ("[a breath, close behind]",
   "[footsteps upstairs]"). For deaf players and laptop speakers.

8  The microphone (optional, off by default: Options > Microphone).
   - Asked once (Options), with a level check ("say something").
   - Loudness as a noise: a gasp or a shout carries like a dropped object
     (NoiseEvent "voice", its reach tunable); quiet talk doesn't.
   - Rarely (at most once a night, from Night 2), it plays back the last
     loud moment it heard (the last ~2 s kept in memory, never saved),
     slightly detuned, from another room.
   - Without a microphone (or permission refused, or the web without
     https) everything works as now.

Each step: tests (tests/round8_test.gd ...), then all suites.
