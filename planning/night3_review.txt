    godot --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 --script res://tools/night3_sim.gd -- <strategy> <seed>
(SIM_TRACE=1 prints every state change.) A run takes ~20 s.


THE NUMBERS
-----------
  Night: 5 min (00:00 to dawn).
  Speeds: player walk 3.5, sprint 6.5 m/s (stamina ~18 s); the Entity hunts
    at 2.2 (4.2 alerted), charges at 6.0 indoors, 9.0 outdoors. Outdoors you
    can't outrun a charge; indoors only just.
  Sight in the dark: 9 m (3.2 m crouched), 16 m in a lit room, 28 m with
    the flashlight on.
  Fuel: 100. Idle 0.12/s + the faulty porch light 0.3/s + 0.5/s per room
    light: one lit room = 0.92/s, a full tank lasts ~1 min 50 s of 5 min.
  Flashlight: battery 100, 0.5/s on, 1.5/s overcharged (~66 s of
    overcharge). Overcharge = hold F 0.25 s.


RESULTS
-------
  passive  died 3/3, at 1:31-1:41: the first time it gets in, it finds you
           and charges. (Expected: no defence, no hope.)
  dark     survived 3/3, with 38-51 charges a night, the battery at 97%.
  lit      survived 3/3 (the fuel ran out ~3:55, then as "dark").
  Ambushes, fake departures: 0 in every run (they need a player it hasn't
  found; these players are found on its first visit).


WHAT THAT SAYS
--------------
1. The flashlight is an infinite shield indoors. [the main problem]
   An overcharged beam on a charge indoors (both inside) puts it in a
   STANDOFF, not FLEE. The standoff ends after ~1.7 s, it backs off ~4 m,
   goes back to HUNT, sees you again at 3 m and charges again: every 3.5 s,
   all night (51 times), at ~1 battery per charge. It never learns, never
   leaves, never escalates. Night 3 goes from "certain death" (no beam) to
   "no risk at all" (beam), and the loop is repetitive rather than scary.

2. Without the beam it's binary the other way: the first visit kills.

3. The lit room is safe as long as the fuel lasts (~2 of 5 minutes for one
   room); then it's the flashlight again. Fuel works as a pressure, but the
   shield makes it not matter.
