ENDINGS PLAN — REVIEW
=====================

What exists today
-----------------
- EndgameManager: DEATH / ESCAPE_MEDIOCRE / ESCAPE_GOOD. The van's "Escape" picks
  Good or Mediocre from RoutineManager.is_anchor_destroyed.
- Cinematics node (cottage_horror): an AnimationPlayer, DeathCam, MediocreCam and GoodCam.
  The cameras have no positions. Only a "death_drag" animation exists, and it is
  empty. "escape_mediocre" and "escape_good" do not exist.
- Death does NOT go through EndgameManager. JumpscareManager does the face-zoom,
  then shows death_screen.tscn ("Restart from checkpoint"). trigger_death_ending()
  is never called.


BUGS FOUND WHILE READING (worth fixing whatever we do)
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Escaping in the repaired van most likely soft-locks the game. EndgameManager
   plays "escape_mediocre"/"escape_good", which don't exist, then waits for
   animation_finished, which never comes. The player stays frozen and the HUD
   hidden, and the ending screen never appears.
2. The ending fades the Master bus to -80 dB and nothing restores it, so the game
   is probably silent after "Retry" or "Main Menu".
3. There are three different end screens: the HUD panel (escapes),
   ending_screen.tscn (unused death path) and death_screen.tscn (actual deaths).


CHANGES I'D MAKE TO THE PLAN
============================

General: build the cinematics in code instead of hand-keyed animations
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The plan keyframes absolute positions in an AnimationPlayer. But the player can
die anywhere (inside, upstairs, in the forest), and the van, road and totem are
far apart. Keyframed cameras would play in the wrong place. Scripted cameras
(tweens and small shake functions) can start from wherever the player actually is.
They can also check for walls and terrain, and I can test them headlessly (no hangs,
correct order, the ending screen appears). The shots stay the same, only the tool
changes.


1. DEATH — "The Drag"
---------------------
Keep: the black-out after the jumpscare, waking in the dirt, the drag, the hoist.
Change:
 a) Start where the jumpscare ends. The existing face-zoom (3.2 s) already is
    the "0-1.5 s jumpscare sync", so the cinematic begins on the black screen.
 b) Location problem: dying inside the cabin or upstairs means "drag through the
    dirt" and "hoist into a tree" would clip through walls. Use the black-out to
    cut to a fixed spot in the forest (the Entity's "lair" or a big tree near the
    totem). This works from any death location, and it also shows the player
    where the Entity takes its victims.
 c) Show the Entity. While dragged, tilt the camera up so you glimpse its legs
    and back pulling you (by the ankle, out of view), with drag and breathing
    sounds.
 d) Impalement: make it more implied than shown. Hard downward lurch, crunch,
    red vignette, the image pulses, sound cuts out, black. A sharp branch mesh
    in frame for a few frames reads fine. It doesn't need to be graphic to work.
 e) IMPORTANT: death is not really an ending in this game; you restart the
    night. A 10-second cinematic every time gets tiring. Play it in full the
    first time, then a short version (drag, then black) or let the player skip it with
    a key press. The final death (if you want a true "game over" on Night 5)
    could always play the full one.


2. MEDIOCRE ESCAPE — "It Follows"
---------------------------------
Problem: the ending text says the Entity FOLLOWED the player home and they
vanished days later. An ambush that slams into the camera reads as "the Entity
killed you in the van", which contradicts that text and feels like a
death, not an escape.
Suggestion (same misdirection, fits the text):
  0-4 s   Drone shot following the van down the road, headlights on, relief
          music. (The van needs headlights and tail lights. At night without
          them you see nothing in the fog.)
  4-6 s   Cut inside or behind: the tail lights light up the road behind,
          and for a frame the Entity is standing in the road, watching.
  6-8 s   Back to the drone shot: something dark is now clinging to the van's
          roof or back, riding along. The van drives on, unaware.
  8 s     Fade. Text: "...it followed the player home."
Needs: a drivable route out of the map (I can lay out a Path3D along the dirt
road to the edge of the map), van headlights, and an engine or drive sound.


3. GOOD ESCAPE — "Dawn"
-----------------------
Problems with the plan:
 - The totem (weeping willow, ~(103, 85)) is about 200 m from the van (~(-9, -90)).
   A camera next to the totem can't see the van leave, especially in fog.
 - The totem is already destroyed and hidden when you purge it (earlier, often on a
   different night), so "totem sinks during the ending" repeats something the
   player already did.
Suggestion: make the good ending the complete opposite of the mediocre one:
  0-5 s   Dawn. Fog thinning, birdsong (no wind or drone). Static wide shot of
          the cabin as the van pulls away down the road.
  5-8 s   Slow pan to where the totem stood: now an empty patch of dead
          ground, maybe with a faint wisp of ash rising.
  8 s     Slow fade to white or warm black.
The contrast (night, pursued vs. dawn, quiet) makes both endings mean more.
If the escape happens at night, the cinematic can fade in "later", at sunrise.


4. SMALLER THINGS
-----------------
 - Merge the end screens: one EndingScreen for both escapes (title, text,
   Retry/Main Menu), and keep death_screen for deaths (restart night).
 - Stop the Entity, the chase music and the tension drone as soon as a cinematic
   starts, and fade the sound itself rather than the player's Master volume.
 - Show the ending name and maybe "Ending 2 of 3" on the screen. Players like
   knowing other endings exist.
