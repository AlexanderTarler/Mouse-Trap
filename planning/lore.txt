==========================================================================

THE PLACE
  A cabin in Swedish pine forest, end of the 1990s (a Windows 98 terminal,
  Miljöbalken 1998 on the notebook's credentials). No phone signal.
  Built by a logging family ("my grandfather laid the logs"). A forestry
  camp worked the woods until 1974; three men vanished and the crews left.
  Svea Naturinstitut took over the cabin, its generator and the old
  forestry archive terminal (it still boots as SVENSKA
  SKOGSBRUKSARKIVET), and staked the stumps around it with orange tape.
  Season: spring, late April (the dormice should still be asleep; the
  birches are green, none turning). The forest is pine with birch through
  it, boulders left by the ice. East of the barn, the camp's last cut
  (1974): grey stumps, grown up since with young birch and pine, and the
  log pile nobody came back for (ForestDresser.CLEARCUT,
  SiteDresser._dress_clearcut).
  Directions (level -Z is north): Nest Alpha east of the cabin, Nest Beta
  west, Nest Gamma far south-west by the sunken tunnel, the willow in its
  own clearing south-east, the barn north-east, the wreck yard west.

THE WATCHER (the Entity)
  Tall, crowned like an elk ("the horn-crowned watcher"). Anchored to the
  willow (the totem rises out of the ground only at night). Hunts by sound,
  copies sounds (footsteps, knocks, the flashlight's click). A quick flash
  of light enrages it and it charges; a steady flood of light holds it.
  Lit rooms and the porch light are safer ground. It breaks fuses, tears
  out ignition wiring, knocks on windows to draw you away.
  The logging family fed it: straw tied to stakes by the willow, wet with
  blood, every autumn.

THE DORMICE (hasselmus)
  Three colonies, awake weeks before hibernation should end. "Its little
  echoes": each copies one of its traits. Alpha: light (photophobia,
  provoked by the overcharged beam). Beta: watching (approach crouched).
  Gamma: mimicry (answers your sounds). Once the anchor burns, the nests
  are empty.

THE INSTITUTE
  Knows. Calls it a dormouse survey; sends one researcher at a time and
  records how it hunts them ("We're the bait"). Polite, careful about what
  it leaves out: "stay inside after dark", "Stenberg has been reassigned",
  "Any activity near the cabin at night is to be logged, not investigated".
